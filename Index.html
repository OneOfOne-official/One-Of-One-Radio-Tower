The archive

Select a black bar to read what was struck from the record.

Subject: a city boy in a small town. Period: one summer. Hour: 1:00 a.m., every night. The files describe a slow pressure, like a room losing air, built from expectation, rent, noise, other people’s plans. The subject did not report it. He recorded it.

The recordings are soft, hazy and low, as if made so as not to wake anyone. They were assembled into a project that survives in fragments. There is no confirmed author. There is only one of each thing: one voice, one window, one hour.

Recovered material includes the track Make Of It What You Want, a ruling on opinion that declines to be ruled on.
